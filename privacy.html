Privacy Policy

ShroomVerse · Last updated 7 September 2026

The short version. ShroomVerse has no accounts and no login, and Volga Karakus does not run servers that store your personal data. Your game progress is saved on your own device. The app does use third-party services — Google AdMob for ads, the App Store or Google Play for purchases, and Google Firebase for crash reports — and those services collect device information in order to work. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier they use at any time in your device settings.

Who we are

ShroomVerse ("the app", "the game") is published by Volga Karakus. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at volgacan+shroomverse@gmail.com.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no sign-up, no login and no user profile. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone or camera access.

Your game progress — colonies, resources, research and settings — is written to a save file in the app's own private storage on your device. We cannot read it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app. It may be included in your device's own backup if you have that switched on, which is between you, your device and your backup provider.

Advertising identifier

Advertising in the app relies on your device's resettable advertising ID. This is not your name and is not tied to your Google account, and you are in control of it:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset the advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. An app may only use the identifier for tracking after asking your permission; ShroomVerse does not ask, so its ads are served without it.

If you are in a region covered by GDPR or similar rules, you will be shown a consent form the first time the app requests an ad, and you can change that choice later from the app's settings.

Notifications

The app can send you notifications — for example that an attack is inbound, or that a research is ready to start. It asks your permission first, and you may refuse or turn them off later in your device settings without losing any other functionality. Notifications are scheduled on your own device.

Children

ShroomVerse is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at volgacan+shroomverseurgent@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data held about you, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent.

Because we hold no personal data ourselves, the fastest routes are usually direct: uninstalling the app removes the local save, and resetting your advertising ID breaks the link to previously collected advertising data. For data held by Google or Unity, use the controls in their policies linked above. You may also write to us and we will help where we can.

Data retention and transfers

We retain nothing, because we collect nothing directly. Data held by the third parties above is retained under their own policies, and those companies operate globally, so information may be processed in countries other than your own — including the United States — under the safeguards described in their policies.

Security

Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play, so payment details never reach the app. Your save data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the app changes what it collects — for example if online multiplayer with real accounts is added in a future version. The date at the top always reflects the current version, and continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
